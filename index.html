A parliament you live in, with the public watching

Contenders move into a mock parliament and stay for 100 days. They debate, form factions, and travel to real districts to deal with real civic crises.

Every Saturday they vote in secret to nominate two people each. Every Sunday the public decides who goes home. The last contender standing wins ₹5 crore for civic development and a national campaign platform.

What a week looks like

Monday

Alliances. The Speaker sets the agenda and factions lock in their whips.

Tuesday

Floor debates. Motions, bills and timed filibusters earn Political Capital Points.

Wednesday

Press crisis room. A mock national disaster and 90 seconds to answer each hostile question.

Thursday and Friday

Field missions. Forty-eight hours in a real district. The winners earn House Immunity.

Saturday

Secret ballot. Everyone without immunity names two contenders to nominate.

Sunday

Live eviction. The public votes and the lowest-ranked contender leaves the house.
