The format

खेल के नियम, एक नज़र में

For channels, platforms and anyone deciding whether this is a show they would watch.

Twenty-six people from across India move into a working mock parliament with 114 cameras and no blind spots. They form factions, debate bills and cut deals. Every week they leave for a real district with a real crisis. Every Sunday, the public sends one of them home.

Twenty-six seats
The house is designed as an 85,000 sq ft purpose-built set on the outskirts of Delhi NCR, with a horseshoe chamber at its centre.
Five factions
Every contender joins a caucus with its own whip and war room, and can defect when the numbers turn.
A weekly sitting
Alliances, floor debates, a press crisis room, field missions, a secret ballot and a live eviction.
Field missions
Forty-eight hours in a real district, with the real people involved, to fix a real problem.
Power you can count
Political Capital Points, public approval and brass immunity tokens decide who is safe.
The public's veto
Viewers can save a leader sunk by backroom plotting, and send the plotter to the danger zone.

The Next Leader is a format in development. It has not been commissioned yet.

Rules of the house

सदन के नियम

Power in the house is counted, not guessed. Points move with every debate and mission, and the public has the last word.

I   POLITICAL CAPITALPOINTS
Complete a field mission+150 to +250
Pass a private member's bill+150
Win a floor debate+50 to +100
Win a hostile press briefing+75
Buy a veto amendment in committee−100
Walk out, or break the ethics code−150

Every contender carries a live balance, usually between 800 and 1,600.

II

Public approval

A rating from 0 to 100%, from the viewer polling booth and weekly national surveys. A high rating unlocks the People's Mandate Shield.

III

Immunity

Heavy brass tokens won in missions and challenges. Played before results are read, a token cancels every vote against its holder or a chosen ally.

IV

Secret ballot

The three or four contenders with the most Saturday votes go on the Nomination Block.

V

Janta Ka Veto

If the public believes a leader was plotted out, it can save them. The whip behind the plot takes their place.

VI

The Speaker's prerogative

The Speaker keeps one power in reserve: the right to save a nominee from Sunday's vote.

Questions

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल
Is The Next Leader on TV?

Not yet. It is a format in development and has not been commissioned. This page explains the idea and lets people register their interest.

Is registering the same as auditioning?

No. Registering tells the team you would like to be considered if auditions open. Nobody is being selected through this page.

Is it a real election?

No. The parliament is a set and the votes are part of the show. The field missions are designed to take place in real districts, with the real people affected.

Do I have to pick a faction?

No. Choose "Not decided yet" if you are unsure. In the format, contenders can cross the floor once they are in the house.

What would the winner get?

As the format stands: ₹5 crore in civic development capital, a national campaign platform, and the title of The Next Leader.

Who sees my details?

Only the show's team. Your answers go to a private sheet and are never shown on this site.
