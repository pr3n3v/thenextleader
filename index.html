The rules of the house

Power in the house is counted, not guessed. Points move with every debate and mission, and the public has the final word.

SEASON 1 RULEBOOK

Articles of the House

सदन की नियमावली
I

Political Capital Points

Every contender carries a live balance, usually between 800 and 1,600. It rises and falls with what they do on the floor and in the field.

Complete a field mission+150 to +250
Pass a private member's bill+150
Win a floor debate+50 to +100
Win a hostile press briefing+75
Buy a veto amendment in committee−100
Walk out, or break the ethics code−150
II

Public approval

A rating from 0 to 100%, drawn from the viewer polling booth and weekly national surveys. A high enough rating unlocks the People's Mandate Shield.

III

Immunity

Heavy brass tokens, won in missions and challenges. Played before the results are read, a token cancels every vote against its holder, or against an ally they choose.

IV

Secret ballot

Every Saturday each contender without immunity names two others for eviction, with reasons on record. The three or four with the most votes go on the Nomination Block.

V

Janta Ka Veto

If the public believes a leader was sunk by backroom plotting, it can save them. The whip who organised the plot takes their place in the danger zone.

VI

The Speaker's prerogative

The Speaker keeps one power in reserve: the right to save a nominee from Sunday's vote.

Questions

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल
Is this a real election?

No. The parliament is a set and the votes are part of the show. The field missions are real, though. They take place in real districts, with the real people affected.

Do I have to pick a faction now?

No. Choose NOTA on the ballot unit if you are not sure yet. Contenders can also switch factions once they are in the house.

How long would I be in the house?

Up to 100 days, if you survive every Sunday.

Where is the house?

The Capitol House is a purpose-built compound on the outskirts of Delhi NCR.

What does the winner get?

₹5 crore in civic development capital, a national campaign platform, and the title of The Next Leader.

What happens after I apply?

You get a reference number on screen straight away. The casting team reviews every nomination and contacts shortlisted applicants on WhatsApp or email.

Who sees my details?

Only the casting team. Your answers go to a private sheet and are never shown on this site.
